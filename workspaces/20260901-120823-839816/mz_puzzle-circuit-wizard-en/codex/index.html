How To Play

- Select a gate in the left toolbox and click an empty grid cell to place it.

- Click Wire, then click an output dot (right side of a gate or input terminal), then an input dot (left side of a gate or output terminal).

- Run Test to watch signals flow across several steps. If your outputs match the target at every step, you win.

- Click an input dot again to remove its wire. Use Erase to delete a gate.

Tips: NOT inverts, AND needs two inputs, XOR is exclusive-or, DEL delays the signal by one step.
